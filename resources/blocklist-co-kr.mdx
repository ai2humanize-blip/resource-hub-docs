### How Blocklists Work: A Technical Overview

At their core, blocklists are databases that contain lists of IP addresses, domains, and other identifiers associated with malicious activities. When a user tries to access a website or receive an email, the system checks these identifiers against the blocklist. If a match is found, the access is blocked, preventing potential harm.

Despite their advantages, blocklists face several challenges. One of the main issues is the constant evolution of cyber threats, which requires blocklists to be continuously updated and refined. Additionally, there is a risk of false positives, where legitimate entities are mistakenly blocked.
